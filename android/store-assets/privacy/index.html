Privacy Policy

Al-Thaqalayn: Quran & Tafsir - Android

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Al-Thaqalayn for Android does not collect any of your data. There is no account and no sign-up. We run no servers, so there is nowhere for your information to go. Everything you read, bookmark, and complete stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no crash reporting. We have no way to receive, see, or store anything about you or how you use the app.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves the following locally, so it can remember your place and your preferences. This information never leaves your phone:

  • The first name you optionally enter, used only to greet you in the app
  • Bookmarked verses
  • Reading progress, streaks, badges, and your sawab count
  • Quiz results and journey progress
  • Settings: theme, language, reading text size, and chosen reciter
  • Notification preferences
  • Whether you have purchased the premium unlock

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, erases all of it permanently. You can also reset your reading progress from within the app, in Settings.

Network connections the app makes

The app works fully offline. All Quran text and commentary ships inside the app itself. It reaches the internet in only two situations:

  • Quran recitation audio. When you press play, audio is streamed on demand from public recitation archives, currently everyayah.com and mp3quran.net. Like any website you visit, those servers can see the standard details of the request, such as your IP address and which audio file was asked for. We do not operate or control those servers, we send them nothing about you, and we receive nothing back from them beyond the audio itself. If you never play audio, the app makes no such requests.
  • The premium unlock. The one-time purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Payment happens inside Google Play, not in our app. We never see your payment details, and the only thing the app learns is whether the unlock has been purchased. Google's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Notifications

Notifications, including the daily verse, are created and scheduled entirely on your device. The app uses no push service, so no message is ever sent to your phone from us or from anyone else. Notifications are off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings or in Android Settings.

Android device backup

Android's built-in backup feature may copy the app's settings to your own Google account, along with those of your other apps. This is done by the Android operating system, not by us. It is your backup, in your account, and we have no access to it. You can disable it in Android Settings under Backup.

What we never do

  • We do not ask for or access your location
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, or microphone
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, and the app shows no ads
  • We do not profile or track you across apps or websites
  • We do not sell, trade, or share your information, because we do not have any

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for users of all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. If the app ever begins collecting data, which is not planned, we will say so here clearly and in advance.
