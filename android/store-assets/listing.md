## Full description (4000 chars max)

The Quran, five layers deep.

Al-Thaqalayn is a Quran companion built around one idea: understanding grows
in layers. Every verse of all 114 surahs carries a five-layer commentary, so
you can go exactly as deep as you want to go:

1. Foundation - simple explanations, historical context, and why the verse
   matters today
2. Classical Shia - the heritage of Tabatabai's al-Mizan and classical
   scholarship
3. Contemporary - modern insights and connections to daily life
4. Ahlul Bayt - narrations from the Prophet and his purified household
5. Comparative - how different schools of thought read the same verse

READ AND LISTEN
- Complete Quran with Arabic text and translation in English or Urdu
- Commentary in English, Urdu, and Arabic
- Verse-by-verse recitation with six renowned reciters
- Read-aloud commentary with sentence highlighting
- Adjustable reading text size

UNDERSTAND
- Quick Gems: tap any verse for its key concepts and core insight
- In-Depth: the full five-layer commentary, one tap away
- Surah quizzes with understanding levels, from Seeker to Sage
- Deep Dives: cinematic guided descents through themes like certainty and
  patience, from the Quran to Karbala
- Inside the Surah: immersive journeys through individual surahs

LIVE THE YEAR
- Seasonal journeys for Ramadan, Hajj, Muharram, Fatimiyya, and Arbaeen,
  with a dua, verses, and reflection for every day
- Daily verse notifications in your language
- Daily challenge, daily crossword, and dua of the day
- Life moments: what to recite for gratitude, anxiety, travel, and more

TRACK YOUR GROWTH
- Reading progress across the whole Quran
- Streaks, badges, and a sawab counter
- Everything stored on your device: no account, no sign-up, fully offline

Start free with Surah al-Fatiha, the first journey day, and the first Deep
Dive. One affordable one-time unlock opens all 114 surahs forever: no
subscription, no ads, ever.

Thaqalayn means "the two weighty things": the Quran and the Ahlul Bayt.
This app is an effort to keep them together, in your pocket.
